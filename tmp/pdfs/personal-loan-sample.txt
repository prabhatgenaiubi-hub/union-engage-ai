To: All the offices/ RLPs/ Branches

       MASTER CIRCULAR ­ UNION PERSONAL LOAN

Banks Personal Loan covers the following scheme:

    1. Personal Loan for Salaried Individual other than Government Employees
    2. Personal Loan for Non-Salaried Individuals
    3. Personal Loan for Government Employees- SRLGE
    4. Union Women Professional Personal Loan
    5. Union Professional Personal Loan
    6. Union Ashiyana Personal Loan Scheme
    7. Union Ashiyana Personal Loan Overdraft Scheme
    8. Union Suraksha Personal Loan

The revamped master circular of Union Personal Scheme was issued by the Bank vide
IC 1910-2020 dated 12.03.2020. Union Personal scheme has undergone various
modifications after issuance of said master circular during the last financial years.
Hence, to make available all / guidelines of this scheme at one place, this revised
Master circular has been compiled.

This master circular supersedes all earlier circulars related to Union Personal
Scheme.

All Offices/Branches are requested to take a careful note of the above and be guided
accordingly.

1.2. Loans should not be given be for speculative purpose.
1.3. Takeover of personal loan from other banks/FIs is not permitted.

3. AGE:

3.1. Minimum entry age of the applicant should be 18 years.
3.2. Maximum entry age of the applicant should be one year prior to retirement age.

4. CREDIT SCORE:

4.1. The credit history reports from credit rating companies such as
     CIBIL/Experian/Equifax should be taken for the applicant & guarantors and should
     form the part of due diligence/appraisal. The report should be compiled from at
     least one credit information company. The report can also be compiled from
     information of more than one company, if the sanctioning authority, so desires.

4.2. In case of any adverse credit history, the sanctioning authority to decide the loan
     on case-to-case basis depending on its merits.
